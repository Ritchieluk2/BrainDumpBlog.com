# Brain Dump: Brand Direction

Chosen direction: **E · Dawn Orbit** (Nov 2, 2026). The live version of every value below is in `app/assets/css/tokens.css` and `app/utils/brand.ts`. See `/styleguide` in the running site.

## Feel
Refined and simple. A sparse, elegant layout on a near-white ground, with bold, saturated color used for the planets and mood icons. Planets are matte, with a crisp diagonal shadow line across each one and a soft shadow cast on the ground below. Tone sits between calm and playful: warm, friendly, and lightly self-aware about the name.

## Color
- Ground "Chalk" #FAF9F7, cards #FFFFFF
- Ink #221D1A, secondary text #55504B, muted text #6B645D, hairlines #ECE8E2
- Planets (lit side / shadow side):
  - terracotta #E2614A / #B8432F
  - marigold #F2A93B / #CC8418
  - saffron #F6CF4A / #D6A91E
  - teal #1FA39B / #127B75
  - cobalt #2F5BEA / #1E3FB5
  - plum #8A4FBF / #673495
  - magenta #D94F8C / #AE3369
- Sunrise accent: marigold, used sparingly (date glyph, logo arc)

## Type
- **Outfit** (300–500) for the brand, headings and interface
- **Fraunces** with the Soft axis for the user's writing, and in italic for accent words ("Empty your *head.*")

## Logo
A terracotta planet rising over an ink horizon line, with a faint marigold sunrise arc and a small cobalt moon. The wordmark is "brain" in Outfit and "dump" in Fraunces italic.

## Mood icons
| Mood | Shape | Color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| joyful | star | saffron |
| calm | sphere | cobalt |

| focused | triangle | marigold |
| tired | crescent | plum |
| hopeful | rounded square | teal |
| tender | sphere | magenta |

## Planets as entries
Each brain dump becomes a planet. Size reflects word count, color reflects the strongest feeling, a moon shows the second-strongest feeling, and a ring marks anxiety. A week of dumps orbit together as a small solar system.

## Components
Pill buttons in ink with a soft cast shadow. Cards use a 28px corner radius with a long, soft shadow. Hairline borders, generous whitespace.

## Rejected or parked
- Glossy planets (round 2, option D): too clowny and childish
- Pastel palettes (round 1): too pale
- Dark-first "Mind Weather": parked; could come back as a dark mode
