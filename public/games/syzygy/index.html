How to play

Every card has four features. Pick three cards where each feature is either the same on all three or different on all three. That's a Syzygy, three bodies in line.

The four features

Examples

Today's puzzle

The twelve cards split into exactly four trios. Some other valid trios are hiding in the grid as decoys. Finding a decoy costs nothing, but it won't solve the puzzle. A wrong trio costs one of your four mistakes.

Race

Play through a full deck. A trio is checked the moment you pick the third card. Blitz gives you three minutes. Blitz +30 adds thirty seconds for each trio. Time trial has no clock pressure: clear the deck as fast as you can. Your best score in each mode is saved on this device.

Flux

Every day, Flux picks four features (color every day, plus number or size, body or phase, and one of ring, glow, moons or sky) and adds two modifiers, such as rainbow wild cards or double points for trios in a straight line. The rule is the same: each feature all the same or all different. You get 3 minutes to score as many points as you can.

Super syzygy

In Flux and Adventure you can select six, nine or twelve cards and submit them together. If they split into valid trios, the whole submission is multiplied by the number of trios. Selecting every card on the table earns a bonus on top.

Adventure

Three acts of three encounters, each with a target score and a clock. Cards are worth 10 points, and a trio scores its points times its mult. Every trio also adds time: 15 seconds times its mult. Redeals and extensions carry over between encounters. Upgrade your deck after each win, and pick constellations that change how scoring works. Each astronaut brings their own four features and a passive ability tied to one of them.

What we save

When you finish a game we save its results, like scores, times and which rewards you picked, to help balance the puzzles. It’s tied to an anonymous id in this browser, or to your account if you’re signed in. Never your writing, name or email. Deleting your account deletes your game history.
